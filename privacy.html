Privacy Policy

App: Arrow Brain Puzzle (com.reotech.arrow_brain_puzzle)
Developer: REO Technologies • Effective Date: August 16, 2026

Summary: REO Technologies ("we," "us," or "our") built Arrow Brain Puzzle as an ad-supported and free-to-play mobile puzzle game. This page informs players regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of personal or technical information when using our application.

1. Information We Process

Arrow Brain Puzzle is designed to be enjoyed without requiring you to create a personal account, sign in with email, or provide your real name. However, to deliver game functionality, save puzzle progress, provide hints, serve advertisements, and diagnose app stability, third-party software development kits (SDKs) may automatically process certain technical data.

2. Device and Technical Information

When you install and open the game on your Android device, the app and its integrated service providers may automatically collect:

  • Device Identifiers: Android Advertising ID (GAID / AAID), unique device tokens, or vendor identifiers.
  • Hardware & System Details: Device model, manufacturer, operating system version (e.g., Android 13/14), screen resolution, and system language.
  • Network Information: General IP address (used to infer country/city-level location for localized ads and network diagnostics), connection type (Wi-Fi, 4G, 5G).

3. App Activity & Game Progress

Game state data—such as completed puzzle levels, current coin balance, earned trophies, active daily challenge streaks, sound preferences, and hint usage—is stored locally on your device using Android local storage (e.g., SharedPreferences). If cloud backup is enabled on your device via Google Play Services, this state may sync according to your Google account settings.

4. Advertising & Google AdMob

Arrow Brain Puzzle displays advertisements to keep the game free for all players. We use Google AdMob and Google Play Services to serve banner ads, interstitial ads, and rewarded video ads.

Rewarded Ads:

Rewarded video ads are completely voluntary. When you choose to watch a rewarded video ad (for example, to earn bonus coins or extra hints), the advertising network processes your interaction to confirm completion before issuing your in-game reward.

Google AdMob may use device advertising identifiers and cookies/mobile identifiers to serve personalized ads (based on your interests) or non-personalized ads (contextual), depending on your consent and device settings.

Review Google's Privacy Policy at: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

5. Analytics & Crash Reporting (Firebase)

Where enabled in the app, we utilize Google Firebase services (such as Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics) to understand gameplay balance and resolve technical bugs:

  • Analytics: Aggregated statistics such as level completion rates, session duration, and feature adoption.
  • Crash Reporting: Stack traces, crash logs, and device state at the moment of an error, allowing us to quickly release bug-fix updates.

6. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

If you make optional in-app purchases (such as purchasing hints, coins, or removing non-rewarded ads), all payment processing is handled exclusively and securely by Google Play Billing. We never receive, store, or process your credit card numbers, banking details, or billing address.

7. Consent & Google UMP (User Messaging Platform)

For players in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom (UK), and Switzerland, we integrate Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent SDK to adhere to the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and ePrivacy Directive. You can review or modify your consent choices at any time through the in-game Settings menu.

8. Data Retention

Locally stored game data remains on your device until you uninstall the app or clear its application cache/data in Android Settings. Third-party partners (such as Google AdMob and Firebase) retain anonymized technical and analytics logs according to their respective standard retention schedules.

9. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. Data is only shared with certified third-party service providers essential for operating the game:

  • Google Play Services: Core platform support and billing.
  • Google AdMob: Ad serving and rewarded ad verification.
  • Google Firebase: Analytics and crash reporting (if enabled).

10. User Choices & Opt-Outs

You have control over how your data and device identifiers are used:

  • Reset or Delete Advertising ID: On Android devices, navigate to Settings → Google → Ads to reset or delete your Advertising ID.
  • Personalized Ads Opt-Out: You can opt out of personalized ad targeting directly in Android settings or via the in-game consent dialog (where applicable).
  • App Data Deletion: You can clear all game data at any time by going to Android Settings → Apps → Arrow Brain Puzzle → Storage → Clear Data.

11. Children's Privacy

Arrow Brain Puzzle is designed for general audiences and puzzle enthusiasts of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age threshold in your country). If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately, and we will take prompt steps to remove such records.

12. Data Security

We value your trust and implement commercially reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect data against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. All communication with third-party service APIs occurs over encrypted HTTPS connections.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates, new features, or changing legal standards. The updated date at the top of this page will reflect the latest version. We encourage players to review this page occasionally.
