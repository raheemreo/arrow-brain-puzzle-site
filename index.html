Game Rules & Strategy

How to Play Arrow Brain Puzzle

Simple to learn, deeply engaging to master. Spatial logic, pathfinding, and foresight come together.

🎯 Core Objective

Fill the entire board with a single continuous line, moving from the Start cell (🟢) to the Exit cell (🏁), visiting every valid arrow cell exactly once.

1
🟢

Start at Green Cell

Your continuous path always begins at the designated emerald Start tile.

2
🗺️

100% Grid Coverage

Visit every single valid arrow cell on the grid. No tiles can be skipped.

3
🚫

No Retracing / Cross

Visit each tile only once. Moving over an already visited cell is forbidden.

4
🏁

Finish at Exit Cell

The very last cell of your unbroken flow line must be the target Exit tile.

5

Orthogonal Moves

Move only horizontally or vertically (Up, Down, Left, Right). No diagonals.

🧩 Board Elements & Legend

🟢
Start Cell (Origin)
The initial starting cell marked with an emerald marker where your path begins.
➡️
Arrow Indicators
Tiles providing directional flow cues to help guide your spatial trajectory.
⬛
Grid Obstacles & Voids
Inactive or blocked tiles that create intricate mazes and cannot be entered.
🏁
Exit Cell (Finish Target)
The designated goal tile where your 100% path must conclude to complete the stage.

🧠 Master Strategies & Pro Tips

🎯 1. Identify Forced Corner Moves First
Corner tiles with only one entrance and exit must follow those exact neighbors. Route these early!
🏁 2. Work Backwards from Exit
Identify which tile must precede the Exit so you don't accidentally step on it too early.
🏝️ 3. Avoid Isolated "Islands"
Never cut off groups of unvisited cells without remaining entry and exit routes. Keep open tiles connected.
↶ 4. Use Undo (↶) Over Full Restarts
Retract 2–3 moves instead of restarting the entire board to stay on track for a 3-star rating.
