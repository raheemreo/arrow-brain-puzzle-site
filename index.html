Game Rules

How to Play in 5 Simple Steps

Simple to learn in seconds, endlessly deep to master over hundreds of stages.

1

Tap an Arrow

Select any arrow on the board that you want to move.

2

Follow the Direction

The arrow will attempt to move forward in its designated pointing direction.

3

Ensure Clear Path

Make sure no other arrows or obstacles block its exit route from the grid.

4

Clear the Board

Remove all arrows sequentially to beat the level and unlock the next challenge.

5

Use Hints When Stuck

Activate hints to reveal the next optimal move whenever you face a tricky grid.
