Interactive Guide & Rules

How to Play Arrow Brain Puzzle

Simple to learn in seconds, endlessly rewarding to master. Explore the core rules, playable demo, and pro strategies below.

🎯 Core Objective

Fill the entire board with a single continuous line, moving from the Start cell (🟢) to the Exit cell (🏁), visiting every valid arrow cell exactly once.

1

Start at Green Cell (🟢)

Your continuous path always begins at the designated emerald Start origin tile.

2

100% Grid Coverage (🗺️)

Visit every single valid arrow cell on the board. No tiles can be skipped.

3

No Retracing or Crossing (🚫)

Visit each cell exactly once. Moving back over visited tiles or crossing paths is forbidden.

4

Finish at the Exit Cell (🏁)

The very last cell of your unbroken flow line must be the target Exit tile.

5

Orthogonal Moves Only (📐)

Move only horizontally or vertically (Up, Down, Left, Right). No diagonal steps allowed.

🎯 1. Identify Forced Corner Moves First

When a corner or edge tile has only one way in and one way out, your path must enter and exit through those exact neighbors. Spot these early to establish firm anchors for your solution.

✓ Anchor corners before drawing long loops

🏁 2. Work Backwards from the Exit Tile

Look at the Exit (🏁). How many adjacent open cells connect to it? Knowing which tile must immediately precede the Exit prevents you from accidentally closing off the exit route too early.

✓ Protect the Exit predecessor tile

🏝️ 3. Avoid Isolated "Island" Cells

When drawing a line through the center of the grid, make sure you don't cut off a group of unvisited cells so they have no remaining entry/exit points. The remaining open cells must always form a connected shape.

✓ Never slice the board into disconnected pockets

↶ 4. Use Undo (↶) Over Full Restarts

When you realize you've created a dead-end, tap Undo 2 or 3 times to back out of the trap rather than resetting the whole board. This saves time and keeps you on track for a 3-star rating!

✓ Smart retractions preserve your star score
