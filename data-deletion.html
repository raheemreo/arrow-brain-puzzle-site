User Data Rights

Data Deletion Policy & Request

App: Arrow Brain Puzzle (com.reotech.arrow_brain_puzzle)
Developer: REO Technologies • Last Updated: August 16, 2026

Your Privacy Rights: REO Technologies respects your right to control your personal data. In compliance with the Google Play User Data Policy, GDPR, and global data privacy regulations, this page explains how you can delete your data stored locally or request the removal of any associated third-party service identifiers.

1. Overview of Data in Arrow Brain Puzzle

Arrow Brain Puzzle does not require user account registration, passwords, email logins, or personal profile creation. The game primarily processes two types of data:

  • Local Gameplay Data: Level progression, star ratings, coin balance, high scores, hint inventory, daily challenge streaks, and sound settings stored locally on your Android device.
  • Third-Party Technical Identifiers: Pseudonymous advertising identifiers (Android Advertising ID / GAID), diagnostics, crash logs, and analytics processed by certified SDKs (Google AdMob and Google Firebase).

2. Method 1: Instant Self-Service Deletion (On-Device)

Because your gameplay data is stored locally on your device, you can completely and instantly delete 100% of your gameplay history, coin balances, and settings at any time without needing to contact us:

📱 Steps to Clear All App Data on Android:

  1. Open your device's Settings app.
  2. Navigate to Apps (or Apps & Notifications).
  3. Select Arrow Brain Puzzle from your installed apps list.
  4. Tap on Storage & Cache.
  5. Tap Clear Storage (or Clear Data) and confirm.
  6. (Optional) Uninstall the app from your device to remove all cached assets.

3. Method 2: Resetting Advertising & Tracking Identifiers

To disassociate your device from personalized ad profiles or reset your advertising history across all Google Play apps:

  • On Android: Open Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Tap Reset advertising ID (or Delete advertising ID to opt out of interest-based ads entirely).

4. Method 3: Submit a Formal Data Deletion Request

If you have contacted our support team previously or wish to submit a formal request to purge any associated diagnostic logs, analytics records, or email correspondence from our systems, you can email our Data Protection team directly:

✉ Submit Data Deletion Request
Quick-Copy Email Template:
To: reodevelopers@gmail.com Subject: Arrow Brain Puzzle - Data Deletion Request Dear REO Technologies Support, I am requesting the deletion of all data associated with my use of Arrow Brain Puzzle. Request Details: - App Name: Arrow Brain Puzzle - App Package: com.reotech.arrow_brain_puzzle - Device Model (optional): [e.g. Samsung Galaxy S23] - Advertising ID (optional, found in Android Settings > Google > Ads): [Insert GAID if available] - Reason / Additional Comments: [Please delete all diagnostic and support records associated with this inquiry] Thank you.

5. Request Processing & Turnaround Time

Upon receiving your email request:

  • Verification: We will review your request within 2 to 3 business days.
  • Deletion Turnaround: All associated support correspondence and records held by REO Technologies will be permanently deleted within 30 calendar days.
  • Confirmation: We will send a confirmation email once your data deletion request has been fulfilled.

6. What Happens After Deletion

Please note that once local storage is cleared or a data deletion request is processed:

  • All level progress, star ratings, earned trophies, and high scores will be permanently reset.
  • Any unspent virtual coins or hints cannot be recovered or restored.
  • Non-consumable purchases (such as "Remove Ads") can be restored at any time by tapping Restore Purchases in the in-game Settings while signed into the same Google Play account.
